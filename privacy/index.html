Privacy Policy

Effective: September 23, 2026

QUSAI EID Carousel Publisher connects to TikTok only after a user grants permission through TikTok Login Kit.

Data processed

The service processes the authorized TikTok account identifier, display name, avatar, granted scopes, access and refresh tokens, carousel images, captions, publishing choices, and publish-status responses.

Purpose

Data is used only to show the connected account, prepare creator-approved photo carousels, publish approved content, refresh authorization, and display publishing status.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored server-side and encrypted. Client secrets and refresh tokens are never exposed in the browser. Public carousel images are retained only as needed for TikTok to retrieve them.

Sharing

Approved content and the minimum required metadata are shared with TikTok when the creator presses Publish. Data is not sold.

Control and deletion

Users may revoke access in TikTok settings and may request deletion of stored authorization and draft data through the operator of this service.

Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, use the contact channel provided by QUSAI EID.
